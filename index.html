Privacy Policy

DNA Convergence apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information from the people who use them. The apps have no user accounts, no tracking, no advertising, and no analytics.

The research data shown in each app is built into the app itself. Any files you choose to open or save stay on your own computer.

Some apps load map images from an online map service. When this happens, that service receives the standard information any website receives, such as your internet address, in order to send the map images. The apps do not send it any other information.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
